VL Bon Map Generator

6 letter-strips (A–F) × 5 boxes = 30. Belt looters get a fixed number of boxes each; everyone gets a contiguous strip. Copy output is Discord-ready markdown.

How it works

Boxes on the map

  • The map has 30 boxes: 6 strips (A–F) of 5, laid out as A/B (top), C/D (middle), E/F (bottom).
  • Belt looters each get a fixed number of boxes — the Boxes per belt looter setting.
  • The remaining boxes are split as evenly as possible among the other bonners.
  • Each person’s boxes are assigned as a compact cluster, kept close and reachable via the center ropes — nobody gets boxes stranded on opposite sides of a level.

Belts

  • 6 belts are split as evenly as possible among the belt looters (the Belts list shows each looter’s name once per belt they receive).

Raffle & NX eligibility

  • NX has 6 slots (up to 6 winners); the raffle has 1 winner.
  • Belt looters cannot win the raffle or NX.
  • Among the remaining bonners, anyone who received more than the average number of boxes (i.e. got an extra box from an uneven split) also cannot win the raffle or NX.
  • The raffle winner is removed from the NX pool — nobody can win both the raffle and NX.
  • Winners are drawn only from the eligible bonners. If fewer than 6 are eligible, NX fills only those slots; if nobody is eligible, the section shows “—”.
